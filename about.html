The PV Tree Crew
Our Story

Born and Raised in Ponte Vedra

"We treat every property like our own. If we wouldn't leave it that way in our own yard, we won't leave it that way in yours."

Ponte Vedra Tree was founded with one simple goal: to give homeowners and businesses in Northeast Florida a tree care service they could actually trust — one that shows up on time, communicates clearly, and leaves every job site cleaner than they found it.

We're proud to call Ponte Vedra home. That means we understand the unique challenges of Florida's trees — from tropical storms and salt air to the fast-growing species that can quickly become hazards. Our crew is trained, equipped, and passionate about doing the job right.

Whether you need a single palm pruned or an entire lot cleared, you'll get the same level of professionalism, care, and thorough cleanup on every job — big or small.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

These principles guide how we work — and why our customers keep calling us back.

Integrity

We give honest quotes, do exactly what we promise, and communicate transparently from first call to final cleanup.

Safety

Every job is assessed for risk before we begin. We protect your property, our crew, and your neighbors on every single project.

Quality

We don't cut corners — literally or figuratively. Every cut is made with purpose, every site is left clean, and every customer is left satisfied.

Community

We're your neighbors. We reinvest in Ponte Vedra by hiring locally, supporting our community, and taking pride in the place we all call home.

Sustainability

We care about Florida's natural environment. We dispose of debris responsibly, mulch when possible, and always recommend preservation over removal.

Reliability

When we say we'll be there, we'll be there. On-time arrivals, responsive communication, and no-nonsense scheduling you can count on.
