Tree Trimming & Pruning

Regular trimming and pruning is the foundation of healthy tree care. Properly shaped trees are stronger, more beautiful, and far less likely to drop limbs during storms — protecting your home and family.

Our crew understands proper cut placement, seasonal timing, and how to balance the natural form of each tree while removing what's hazardous or unwanted.

  • Crown thinning & elevation
  • Deadwood removal for safety
  • Clearance from roofs, gutters & fences
  • Storm & wind damage prevention pruning
  • Aesthetic shaping & canopy shaping

Service 03

Palm Pruning

Florida's iconic palm trees are a beautiful part of the landscape — and they require specific care to stay healthy and hurricane-ready. Improper pruning is one of the leading causes of palm decline, so proper technique matters.

We follow best-practice standards for palm pruning, removing only what's necessary to keep your palms thriving through every season.

  • Dead frond removal (brown & yellow)
  • Boot & seed pod cleaning
  • Hurricane prep pruning
  • All palm species serviced
  • Full debris cleanup included

Service 04

Stump Grinding

Old stumps are more than an eyesore — they're a tripping hazard, a magnet for termites and other wood-boring insects, and they take up valuable space in your yard. Our stump grinder eliminates stumps quickly and completely.

We grind stumps below grade level, so you can sod, plant, or build right over the area once we're done.

  • Grinding to below ground level
  • Single stumps or multiple stumps
  • All species and sizes
  • Surface mulch fill included
  • Root exposure addressed
